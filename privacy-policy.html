1. About this policy

SprigDocs is a PDF and image utility for Android, provided by {Muhammad Asher Ansari. It creates PDFs from images, merges and extracts PDF pages, optimizes embedded images in PDFs, exports pages as JPG/PNG, and resizes, compresses or converts supported images.

This policy covers the app and its advertising integration. A website hosting this page may have separate hosting logs or practices; the owner must review those when publishing.

2. Your documents and images

File processing takes place locally. SprigDocs does not upload selected document contents, filenames, thumbnails or generated outputs to a developer server or advertising SDK. Original source files are not modified.

When you choose Open, Share or Save a copy, Android passes your chosen output to the app or document provider you select. A cloud provider or receiving app may transfer it off the device under its own policy. Check your destination before sharing sensitive material.

3. Permissions and local storage

The Android system document picker grants access to files you select. The app does not request broad access to your photo library or shared storage. It keeps private imported copies for the current workspace, preferences such as theme/default formats, and generated outputs indexed in Files. Temporary processing files use private storage.

Imported copies are removed when removed from or replaced in the workspace. Temporary partial outputs are cleaned after errors/cancellation or on restart. Generated files stay until you delete them, clear app storage, or uninstall. App backup/device transfer is disabled. Copies exported elsewhere remain with that destination and must be deleted there separately.

4. Advertising and Google services

Advertising can be enabled in a configured build. Such builds use Google AdMob, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). A build with advertising disabled makes no app advertising requests. Documents are not used for ad targeting.

In an ads-enabled build, Google may collect/share IP address (including approximate location derived from it), advertising/app-set or other device identifiers, ad/app interaction information, and performance diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. This is SDK data collection even when document processing is offline. Google describes SDK transfers as encrypted in transit.

The app has no separately integrated analytics or crash-reporting service. Advertising SDK diagnostics can still be collected when advertising is used. No mediation adapters or rewarded advertising are configured for version 1.0.

See Google's Privacy Policy, Google advertising information and Mobile Ads data disclosure. Google controls its service retention; this app does not promise to delete Google's records.

5. Consent and privacy choices

UMP refreshes consent information when an ads-enabled app starts. Ad requests require UMP eligibility. Depending on regional requirements and publisher configuration, UMP may offer consent, refusal or restricted advertising choices. Refusal does not necessarily mean all advertising is disabled; Google's configured limited-ad behavior can apply.

Settings shows Manage Privacy Choices when UMP requires an entry point. You can also manage/reset/delete your advertising ID in Android settings where supported. Core file operations do not require accepting personalized advertising or a network connection. A failed consent or ad request never locks these tools.

6. Retention, security and your rights

App-private storage, scoped picker access, read-only sharing grants and verified output writes reduce unnecessary exposure. SprigDocs does not add document encryption or a password vault. Device compromise, exported copies and third-party apps remain outside these protections; no method guarantees absolute security.

You can remove generated outputs in Files and clear app data in Android settings. For privacy questions or applicable access/deletion requests, contact the owner below. Requests about third-party service data may also need to go to that provider. No account registration is offered in this app.

7. Audience and children's privacy

SprigDocs has no in-app account, social feed or messaging feature. Parents or guardians can contact the owner with privacy concerns. Any distribution to children requires the owner to review applicable child-protection rules and Google Play/AdMob family settings before release; general consent configuration alone does not certify child-directed use.

8. Updates and contact

The owner may update this policy when functionality, providers or legal requirements change. Review the effective date above. Material changes require appropriate notice as applicable.
